How these units are defined

In this calculator, one square gaj equals one square yard and equals 9 square feet (about 0.836127 square metres). A linear gaj is 3 feet and is used only for plot dimensions. In Haryana and Punjab, one killa equals one acre: 43,560 square feet, 4,046.856 square metres, or about 0.4047 hectares.

Regional land units such as marla, kanal, bigha and kattha can vary by district and local revenue convention. Confirm the applicable unit against your sale deed, jamabandi, or local revenue records before a transaction.
